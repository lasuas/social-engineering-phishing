# Phishing Attack – Social Engineering

## Introduction
Social engineering is a technique used by attackers to manipulate people into giving away confidential information. Instead of attacking systems directly, attackers target human behavior. One of the most common social engineering attacks is phishing.

## What is Phishing?
Phishing is a social engineering attack where an attacker pretends to be a trusted organization, such as a bank or an online service, to trick victims into revealing sensitive information like usernames, passwords, or personal data.

## Example Scenario
In a phishing attack, an attacker sends a fake email that looks like it is from a legitimate bank. The email claims there is a security issue with the user’s account and asks the user to click a link to verify their information. The link leads to a fake website designed to steal login credentials.

## Impact of Phishing
Phishing attacks can result in stolen accounts, financial loss, identity theft, and unauthorized access to personal or organizational systems.

## Prevention
To prevent phishing attacks, users should avoid clicking suspicious links, verify the sender’s email address, enable two-factor authentication, and be cautious of urgent messages requesting personal information.

## Conclusion
Phishing is a serious social engineering threat because it exploits human trust. Increasing awareness and following basic security practices can significantly reduce the risk of falling victim to phishing attacks.
